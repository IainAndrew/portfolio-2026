About

Hello. I'm Iain, a software engineer in Dublin, Ireland. I've spent twelve years building things for the web, the last five and a half of them on Pexels at Canva, where I went from frontend specialist to the engineer the team handed its hardest work to, across web, backend, mobile and data.

I work remotely and intend to keep doing so. Since early 2025 coding agents have been central to how I work: I plan, decompose and review; they write most of the code. There's more on that here.

Before software I was a sound engineer, with a diploma in sound engineering and music production, a few years in Dublin studios, and then a small online mastering business of my own.

I'm available for new roles. The quickest way to reach me is email.
