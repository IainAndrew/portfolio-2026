About

Hi, I'm Iain. I'm a software engineer in Dublin, Ireland. I've been building software for the last twelve years, most recently at Canva. My background is in frontend, and it's still where I'm most at home, but for the last few years I've owned work across backend, mobile and data too.

Over the last couple of years I've built my day-to-day workflow around coding agents, and helped my team at Canva do the same.

I'm currently looking for my next role. Ideally fully remote, on a product I'm excited about, with a team that works closely together and is all in on building with AI. If that sounds like you, drop me an email.

In a previous life I was an audio engineer, spending a few years in Dublin recording studios and running my own online audio mastering business.

I still make music. I write and record sad alternative folk songs under my own name, which you can listen to on Spotify.
