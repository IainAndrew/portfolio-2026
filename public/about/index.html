About

Hi, I'm Iain. I'm a software engineer in Dublin, Ireland. I've been building software for the last twelve years, most recently at Canva. My background is in frontend, though these days I'm just as comfortable working across the rest of the stack.

I work remotely and plan to keep it that way. Over the last couple of years I've built my day-to-day workflow around coding agents, and I've written a bit about how that looks in practice.

In a previous life I was an audio engineer, spending a few years in Dublin recording studios and running my own online audio mastering business.

I still make music. I write and record sad alternative folk songs under my own name, which you can listen to on Spotify.

I'm currently looking for my next role. If you'd like to talk, drop me an email.
